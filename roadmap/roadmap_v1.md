# SafeOption AI Roadmap V1

## Vision

SafeOption AI aims to become a multilingual AI-powered financial risk education platform focused on helping retail investors understand option risk before trading.

The project prioritizes:

* education
* risk awareness
* multilingual accessibility
* responsible investing

---

# Phase 1 — Hackathon MVP (June 2026)

## Primary Goal

Build a minimum viable educational risk analysis platform for the Proof of Usefulness Hackathon.

---

## Core MVP Modules

### 1. Sell Put Risk Calculator

Features:

* break-even analysis
* assignment risk warning
* dividend risk warning
* annualized return estimate

### 2. Buy Call Educational Module
