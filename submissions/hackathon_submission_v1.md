# SafeOption AI — Hackathon Submission V1

## Project Name

SafeOption AI

---

## Tagline

Education First. Risk First.

---

## Project Overview

SafeOption AI is a multilingual AI-powered option risk education platform designed for retail investors and beginner option traders.

The project focuses on helping users understand option risk before trading.

Unlike speculative trading tools, SafeOption AI prioritizes:

* education
* risk awareness
* responsible investing
* multilingual accessibility

---

# Problem Statement

Many retail investors lose money in options trading because they do not fully understand:

* assignment risk
* implied volatility
* dividend risk
* leverage
* break-even structure
* risk / reward imbalance

Most existing trading tools focus on execution rather than education.

SafeOption AI aims to reduce financial learning barriers and improve risk understanding.

---

# Proposed Solution

SafeOption AI provides an educational AI-assisted framework that helps users evaluate option risk before entering trades.

The first MVP includes:

## 1. Sell Put Risk Calculator

Inputs:

* stock price
* strike price
* premium
* expiry date

Outputs:

* break-even analysis
* assignment risk warning
* dividend risk warning
* estimated annualized return

---

## 2. Buy Call Educational Module

Topics include:

* leverage
* time decay
* implied volatility
* risk structure

---

## 3. Multilingual Framework

Initial languages:

* English

Future expansion:

* हिन्दी

# Important Principle

SafeOption AI is NOT:

* an automated trading system
* a broker platform
* a high-frequency trading tool
* a financial advisory service

The project does NOT:

* execute trades
* manage user funds
* guarantee investment returns

---

# Why This Project Matters

Financial education remains inaccessible to many non-professional and non-English-speaking investors.

SafeOption AI aims to create:

* multilingual access
* AI-assisted learning
* responsible investing awareness
* educational-first financial technology
